Our Technology

Powered by Innovation

Four core technologies that define every MYHBeauty instrument.

Radio Frequency

1-3MHz multi-polar RF that penetrates dermis to stimulate collagen production and tighten skin from within.

LED Phototherapy

Medical-grade LEDs in 7 wavelengths (415nm-850nm) for clinical-grade light therapy treatments.

Microcurrent (EMS)

Low-level electrical currents that mirror the body's natural signals to tone muscles and boost ATP production.

Ultrasonic Waves

28-40kHz high-frequency vibration for deep cleansing, gentle exfoliation, and transdermal nutrient delivery.
