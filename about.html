The Beginning

From Workshop
to Global Brand

In 2009, three engineers with a shared passion for beauty technology started MYHBeauty in a modest 200-square-meter workshop. Their vision was radical for its time: bring professional-grade beauty instruments out of exclusive clinics and into the hands of everyone.

What began with a single RF device prototype has grown into a comprehensive portfolio of over 200 product models, serving salons, spas, clinics, and homes across more than 60 countries.

Yet our spirit remains unchanged — obsessive attention to detail, relentless pursuit of innovation, and a deep respect for the people who trust our instruments with their skin.

Milestones

Our Journey

Key moments that shaped MYHBeauty into the brand it is today.

2009

The Foundation

MYHBeauty is founded by three engineers in Shenzhen, China. The first RF skin tightening prototype is completed within six months.

2012

First International Export

Expanded beyond domestic market, shipping our first orders to beauty distributors in Europe and Southeast Asia.

2015

R&D Center Opening

Opened our dedicated 2,000m² research and development facility with in-house EMC and biocompatibility testing labs.

2018

LED Phototherapy Line

Launched our flagship LED light therapy series, quickly becoming a best-seller across 40+ countries.

2021

Smart Connected Devices

Introduced our first app-connected beauty instruments with Bluetooth integration and personalized treatment programs.

2024

Sustainability Commitment

Pledged full transition to recyclable packaging and eco-conscious manufacturing by 2027. Reached 60+ countries served.
