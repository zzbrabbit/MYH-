Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for OEM/ODM? +

Our standard MOQ for OEM/ODM projects is 500 units. For larger or more complex custom designs, we can discuss flexible arrangements based on your specific needs. Contact our B2B team for a detailed quote.

Do your products come with certifications? +

Yes. All MYHBeauty instruments are CE, FDA, and RoHS certified. Additional certifications such as PSE, KC, and TGA can be arranged for specific markets upon request.

What is the warranty period for your devices? +

We offer a 24-month warranty on all professional devices and a 12-month warranty on home-use products. Extended warranty options are available for bulk and OEM orders.

How long does shipping typically take? +

For in-stock items, we ship within 3-5 business days. International shipping takes 7-15 days by sea and 3-7 days by air, depending on destination. OEM orders have a production lead time of 25-35 days.

Can I get a product sample before placing a bulk order? +

Absolutely. We provide sample units for evaluation. Sample costs are credited toward your bulk order if placed within 60 days. Contact us to arrange a sample shipment.
